Evelyn Shen

Managing Partner · FUEL Investment Group

Evelyn is a former healthcare professional turned real estate investor. After years working as a pharmacist, she faced the challenges of long work hours, inflexible schedules that kept her from important family moments, and the financial limitations of a capped salary and slow-growing 401(k).

Determined to find a better way, she transitioned into real estate investing and uncovered the power of passive income to achieve financial freedom. Through her work, Evelyn empowers her investors to reshape their financial futures and create lives they're truly excited about.
