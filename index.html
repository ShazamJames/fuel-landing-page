Who we are

What 10 years of real investing actually looks like.

I'm Evelyn — a former pharmacist turned real estate operator.

Over the last decade I've personally deployed $850K across 8 passive real estate deals. Four performed well. Two resulted in capital loss — and those losses taught me exactly what to look for, what to avoid, and which questions most operators won't answer unless you push. Two are navigating the current market cycle: cash flow positive, capital intact, no distributions yet.

I'm not here to show you a trophy shelf. I'm here to show you what a decade of real investing looks like — and help you avoid the mistakes I made.
